# Sample data provenance
- `sample_cube_ascii.stl`: self-created ASCII STL fragment containing two triangles (one square face), coordinates in arbitrary model units. It is a parser/viewer smoke-test fixture, not a complete watertight cube.
- `sample_temperature.csv`: synthetic nodal temperature sample, not an actual ANSYS export. Coordinates are metres and temperature is degrees Celsius. Schema exactly matches `node_id,x_m,y_m,z_m,temperature_C`.
- No third-party datasets or proprietary CAD/ANSYS files are included.
